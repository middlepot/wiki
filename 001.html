teddy bears


a little creature of cloth, entrusted with the quiet work of comforting a human heart.

formal observations

a teddy bear is a small representation of a bear, constructed primarily from fabric, thread, and soft filling. it is most commonly given to human children and kept in bedrooms, beds, nurseries, and other domestic spaces.

its practical purpose is difficult to establish. it does not provide food, perform household labor, or offer physical protection. nevertheless, humans frequently assign considerable importance to these objects.

unlike most possessions, teddy bears are touched often. they are hugged, carried, slept beside, dressed, repaired, named, and sometimes preserved long after childhood has ended.


field log


entry 001 — a study of companionship

“ the specimen has remained beside my pillow every night. its construction is simple : cloth, thread, soft filling, two glassy eyes. it possesses no detectable awareness, yet i continue positioning it carefully before i leave the room.

i have observed that humans often keep such creatures from childhood onward. their usefulness appears unrelated to function. instead, repeated touch seems to give them a peculiar significance; worn fur and loosened stitches are regarded not as defects, but as evidence of having been loved.

last night, i held mine while falling asleep.

there was no measurable change in temperature, safety, or surroundings. still, i felt distinctly less alone.

i have amended my classification.

previous : toy.

current : object of comfort; possible keeper of affection. ”
