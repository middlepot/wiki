curtains


a delicate veil between home and the world, sewn to dress the daylight.

formal observations

curtains are flexible textile panels suspended over windows, generally intended to regulate the passage of light, provide privacy and contribute to the decoration of human dwellings. their construction varies considerably, ranging from heavy, opaque fabrics to delicate materials through which daylight may pass almost unhindered.

of particular interest are sheer curtains, whose practical effectiveness appears somewhat limited. they do not entirely obstruct the view outside, nor do they offer substantial protection against the elements. nevertheless, humans frequently select them for their softness, translucency and the gentle movement they produce when disturbed by passing air.

the arrangement of fabric around a window also appears to alter the perceived character of a room. sunlight becomes diffused, familiar surroundings acquire a softer appearance, and the boundary between indoors and outdoors seems less abrupt.

it may therefore be concluded that curtains serve not only to conceal or protect, but also to make the presence of the outside world more agreeable within the home.


field log


entry 005 — on the domestication of sunlight

“ i spent part of this morning observing the curtains beside my window.

the fabric was thin enough to allow daylight through, yet it softened the appearance of everything it touched. when a little breeze entered the room, the curtains moved in gentle, irregular intervals. i attempted to determine whether this movement served any useful purpose, but the results were inconclusive.

humans often place beautiful things where they are not strictly necessary. a window functions perfectly well without curtains, and sunlight requires no assistance in entering a room.

still, i found myself feeling rather fond of the little folds of fabric, and the way they made the room seem sheltered without closing it away from the world.

i have therefore resolved to leave the curtains partly drawn whenever the window is open, so that i may observe their gentle movements a little longer. ”
